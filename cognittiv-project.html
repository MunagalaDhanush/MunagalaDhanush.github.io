01 · Overview

What we built and why it mattered

Cognittiv is an AI platform that uses neuroscience-based assessment to measure daily cognitive readiness across industrial workforces — flagging elevated fatigue, coordination strain, and cognitive overload before they result in workplace incidents. Our team was engaged to develop the complete Go-To-Market strategy for Cognittiv's entry into the U.S. Petroleum Refining market, one of the most operationally complex, safety-critical, and risk-averse industrial environments in the world.

The U.S. refining industry processes roughly 18 million barrels of crude oil daily across 132 active refineries. Turnaround and maintenance periods — when 72–90% of the on-site workforce consists of contractors — represent the highest-risk operational windows, and the window where Cognittiv's cognitive readiness platform delivers the most measurable value.

Our task was not to theorize about this market — it was to produce a deployment-ready strategy: who to sell to first, how to size the opportunity, what roles carry the highest risk, how to price for adoption, and how to build trust in a relationship-driven, slow-moving industry where unproven technology faces significant resistance.

03 · Analytical Framework

Nelson Complexity Index (NCI) methodology

The centerpiece of our segmentation framework was the Nelson Complexity Index — a real industry metric used by refiners, investment banks, and energy consultants to quantify the sophistication of a refinery's process unit configuration. We adapted NCI for GTM segmentation: higher complexity means more process units, larger contractor workforces, more turnaround frequency, and higher cognitive load on workers — directly mapping to Cognittiv's value proposition.

We assigned NCI weights per process type (distillation, fluid catalytic cracking, coking, hydrocracking, reforming, etc.), then multiplied by each refinery's BPD (barrels per day) capacity to derive an estimated operational headcount per facility. This gave us a ranked, prioritized list of U.S. refineries segmented by complexity tier — from which our SOM beachhead targets were selected.

04 · Safety & Workforce Research

OSHA injury analysis & high-risk role mapping

We pulled five years of OSHA incident data (2020–2024, NAICS 324110) to identify the top injury types, the roles most exposed, and the beachhead refineries' individual safety incident histories. A Power BI dashboard was built on this dataset to surface trends across injury type, severity, and role function.

We then built a comprehensive in-plant workforce taxonomy covering six functional areas — unit operators, control panel operators, routine maintenance, turnaround maintenance, inbound/outbound logistics, and utilities — and mapped 10+ active contractor agencies (Total Safety, Acuren Inspection, MISTRAS Group, C2O Americas, Stronghold Specialty, and others) operating in the Port Arthur/Beaumont corridor using job posting evidence and industry records.

Key finding: 72–90% of a refinery's turnaround workforce is contractors, hired in short windows with limited site familiarity — the highest-risk population and the core Cognittiv use case.

05 · Go-To-Market Approach

Land-and-expand across three phases

Petroleum refining is relationship-driven, highly risk-averse, and slow to adopt unproven technology. The GTM approach was designed around this reality: enter at low friction, prove value locally, then scale through demonstrated ROI.

Phase 01 · Entry
Individual refinery pilot
90-day pilot/POC at a single beachhead site. Discounted entry pricing. Goal: improved cognitive readiness scores, reduction in near-misses, positive workforce adoption. Build internal champions at plant level.
Phase 02 · Expand
Full-site and multi-site
Convert pilot into full-site deployment. Extend to multiple sites within same operator. Standardize safety and workforce insights across operations. Shift to site-level subscription pricing.
Phase 03 · Scale
Enterprise & industry standard
Corporate-level contracts with large operators. ROI case studies and partner-leveraged expansion. Establish Cognittiv as a category standard in human-performance safety across the industry.

08 · Reflection

What this engagement taught me

This was the first time I worked directly on a deliverable that a real company planned to act on — not a grade, not a simulation. The stakes changed the way I approached every analytical decision. The NCI segmentation methodology forced me to understand industrial engineering concepts I had never encountered, because the client's success depended on the framework being defensible, not just plausible.

Working in the energy sector also taught me something that no data analytics course covers: industry trust is the actual product. The most technically perfect GTM strategy fails if it doesn't account for union dynamics, risk-aversion culture, and the relationship structures that govern how buying decisions actually get made. The pricing architecture, the channel strategy, and the non-punitive legal positioning were all shaped by that insight.

Supervised by Greg Bean, Executive Director of the Bauer Gutierrez Energy Management Institute, this engagement gave me direct exposure to how energy-sector decision-makers evaluate new technology — experience I carry into every analytical role I take forward.
